# 📸 PhotoBooth App

![React](https://img.shields.io/badge/React-20232A?style=for-the-badge&logo=react&logoColor=61DAFB)  
![JavaScript](https://img.shields.io/badge/JavaScript-F7DF1E?style=for-the-badge&logo=javascript&logoColor=black)  

Welcome to your personal web-based photobooth! This app lets you snap three quick photos with a fun countdown, then arranges them into a stylish photo strip you can download and share.

---

## ✨ What It Does
- 📷 Uses your device’s camera for live photo-taking  
- ⏳ Gives you a 3-second countdown so you can strike a pose  
- 🖼️ Creates a vertical photo strip with your three pictures  
- 📱 Looks great and works smoothly on phones and computers  
- 💾 Lets you download your photo strip as an image file  
- 🔄 Includes a reset button so you can start fresh anytime

---

## 🚀 How to Use It
1. Open `index.html` in a modern browser like Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge  
2. Click **Start Camera** and allow access to your webcam  
3. Get ready in front of the camera  
4. Click **Take Photo** and wait for the countdown to snap your picture  
5. Repeat until you have all three photos  
6. Hit **Download Strip** to save your photo strip to your device

---

## 📱 What You Need
- A modern browser with camera support  
- Running on HTTPS or localhost (for development)  
- Camera permissions enabled

---

## 🛠️ How It Works (if you’re curious)
- Accesses your webcam through your browser  
- Captures photos from the live video feed  
- Uses a timer so you get a few seconds to prepare before each snap  
- Combines the photos into a single strip for easy download  
- Everything runs right in your browser—no uploading or fancy servers needed!

---

## 🎨 Design Highlights
- Clean, modern look with smooth animations  
- Responsive layout that works well on any screen size  
- Easy-to-use buttons with fun hover effects

---

## 🔧 Want to Customize?
- Change the countdown length in the JavaScript file  
- Adjust photo sizes and spacing to your liking  
- Update colors, fonts, and animations in the CSS file

---

## 🚨 Troubleshooting Tips
- Make sure you’re using HTTPS or running locally  
- Confirm camera permissions are granted  
- Refresh the page if the camera doesn’t activate  
- Check your browser’s download settings if saving photos fails
